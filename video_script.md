## 2 · Step-by-Step Script with Exact Words to Say

---

### Scene 1 — 0:00 to 0:45 · The Real Problem

**🖥 What to do on screen:**  
Start on the **Fleet overview** page. Point your mouse to the top bar where it says **AIR POWER** and the green **LIVE IoT FEED** dot.

**🎤 What to say (Read aloud):**  
> *"Hello everyone. Today I am presenting AirPower, built for the Ministry of Defence under Problem Statement 26249: Predictive Maintenance and Fleet Availability.  
>  
> In military air fleets, keeping fighter jets ready to fly is a massive challenge. Right now, different teams use different systems: sensor data stays inside the aircraft, maintenance logs are written in paper books, and spare parts are tracked in a separate warehouse system.  
>  
> Because these systems don't talk to each other, maintenance is always reactive. Teams only find out a part is broken after a flight, which grounds the aircraft for weeks while waiting for spare parts to arrive.  
>  
> AirPower solves this problem by connecting all these pieces into one smart, real-time system that predicts failures days before they happen."*

**✍ Quick tip:** Keep a relaxed, confident tone. Pause for 2 seconds after finishing.

---

### Scene 2 — 0:45 to 1:30 · Fleet Overview (Can We Fly Today?)

**🖥 What to do on screen:**  
Move your mouse across the top four metric cards:
- **Fleet availability 75%**
- **Mission capable 80%**
- **Early warning 5.3 days**
- **5,957 hours saved**  
Then scroll down slightly to show the **Fleet availability recovery trend** line graph and the **Downtime Pareto** bar chart.

**🎤 What to say (Read aloud):**  
> *"Right here on the Fleet Overview, the commanding officer gets an instant answer to one simple question: 'How many jets can fly right now?'  
>  
> Instead of calling multiple hangars and depots, everything is visible on one screen. Our fleet availability has reached seventy-five percent—that is seven percent higher than the old baseline. This means more ready aircraft on the runway every single day.  
>  
> Looking at the graph in the middle, you can see our steady recovery over the last twelve months.  
>  
> And the chart at the bottom shows where downtime is coming from. It tells us that engine vibration and hydraulic leaks cause most of the delays, so our teams can focus on fixing them before they cause a grounding."*

**✍ Quick tip:** Point with your mouse directly at the 75% card and the graph curve as you talk about them.

---

### Scene 3 — 1:30 to 2:10 · Aircraft Inventory (Tracking All 20 Jets)

**🖥 What to do on screen:**  
Click **Aircraft inventory** in the left menu.  
Show the grid of 20 aircraft cards. Click the filter buttons at the top (**Airworthy**, **In Maintenance**, **AOG**).  
Then point to jet **AC-02** and click on it to open its digital twin.

**🎤 What to say (Read aloud):**  
> *"Next, let's open the Aircraft Inventory. Here, we track all twenty aircraft across the squadron.  
>  
> Every jet shows its real-time flight status: green means fully ready for missions, yellow means minor servicing, and red means grounded.  
>  
> For each aircraft, we track total flight hours, landing cycles, and when it was last inspected. This keeps everyone on the exact same page, from flight-line mechanics to headquarters.  
>  
> Now, let's click on tail number AC-02 to look inside its digital twin."*

**✍ Quick tip:** Click on `AC-02` smoothly so the screen transitions to the Digital Twin.

---

### Scene 4 — 2:10 to 3:00 · Digital Twin (Live Sensor Stream)

**🖥 What to do on screen:**  
You are now on the **Digital twin** page for `AC-02`.  
Move your cursor over the aircraft drawing. Click on **Turbofan Engine** to show its wear level.  
Scroll down to the **Live telemetry stream** chart and watch the graph update live every 2 seconds.

**🎤 What to say (Read aloud):**  
> *"The Digital Twin gives us a live, interactive model of the aircraft. By clicking on different parts—like the engine, hydraulics, or radar—we can see their health and wear levels immediately.  
>  
> Below the model is our live sensor feed. This is not static data—sensor readings update right here on the screen every two seconds.  
>  
> It tracks live engine temperature, vibration, and hydraulic pressure.  
>  
> Running directly inside the browser is an intelligent anomaly detector. Instead of waiting for a high alarm when it's already too late, it notices tiny shifts in temperature and vibration that human eyes would easily miss, spotting problems days in advance."*

**✍ Quick tip:** Let the viewer watch the line chart draw 2 or 3 new data points while you speak.

---

### Scene 5 — 3:00 to 4:00 · Predictive Faults & The 3-Click Action

**🖥 What to do on screen:**  
Click **Predictive faults** in the left menu.  
Click on the first critical alert (**Turbofan Bearing Spallation on AC-02**).  
Point to **Remaining Useful Life: 3.8 days**.  
Point to the simple bars showing **Why the model flagged it** (High vibration 42%).  
Now perform the 3 clicks slowly:
1. Click **Acknowledge alert**.
2. Click **Raise work order**.
3. Click **Indent spare**.  
Scroll down to show the new entry added to the **Audit trail**.

**🎤 What to say (Read aloud):**  
> *"Now let's go to Predictive Faults. Here, our AI analyzes the sensor data to forecast failures.  
>  
> On this top alert for AC-02, the AI predicts that an engine bearing will fail in just 3.8 days.  
>  
> It also clearly explains why: excessive vibration accounts for forty-two percent of the risk, along with higher exhaust temperature. There are no mysterious black boxes here.  
>  
> But predicting a failure is only half the battle. You have to take action immediately. Watch how easy AirPower makes this in just three clicks:  
>  
> First, we acknowledge the alert.  
> Second, we click Raise Work Order, which sends the repair job directly to the hangar.  
> Third, we click Indent Spare, which orders the replacement bearing from the warehouse.  
>  
> Everything is saved into an automatic audit log showing who did it and when."*

**✍ Quick tip:** Make sure each button clicks cleanly and changes to green before moving to the next one.

### Scene 7 — 4:40 to 5:20 · Spares & Stores (Never Run Out of Parts)

Show the parts list. Point out the **100% Spares Fill Rate** card at the top.

**🎤 What to say (Read aloud):**

>  
> AirPower fixes this by comparing how many parts we have in stock against how many days it takes for a supplier to deliver new ones. If stock gets too low, it raises an automatic warning.  
>  
> Here is the engine bearing we ordered two minutes ago. The warehouse officer can approve the order with one click, and mark it received as soon as it arrives at the depot.  
>  
> Because parts are ordered before the jet even lands for servicing, our spares fill rate is at one hundred percent, meaning zero planes are stuck waiting for parts."*

**✍ Quick tip:** Clicking Approve Indent turns the status green—point your mouse at it.

## 3 · 4 Simple Tips for a High Score Video

1. **Speak Slowly and Clearly:** You have plenty of time. 880 words across 7 minutes means you never need to rush. Take a breath between scenes.
2. **Move the Mouse With Purpose:** Move directly to a button, stop, click, and wait 1 second so the camera captures the change.
3. **Use Headset or Mic:** Clear audio makes your presentation sound 10 times more professional.
4. **Practice Once:** Do a single practice run with a stopwatch before recording. If you finish between 6:40 and 7:00, it is perfect!
